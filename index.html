ENTERPRISE PARTNERSHIPS / PROVE THE FIT

A partner is only as good
as the workflow it improves.

I would apply the same evidence standard to AI vendors, platform providers, agencies, consultancies, and emerging technology partners.

  1. Define the use case

    Business owner, user need, baseline, target behavior, and where the partner adds something the existing ecosystem cannot.

  2. Pass the controls

    Security, privacy, source rights, data handling, human review, and auditability—validated with the relevant experts.

  3. Run a bounded pilot

    Agreed success criteria, integration effort, operating cost, adoption plan, and explicit exit conditions.

  4. Decide scale, revise, or stop

    Review the evidence, assign ongoing ownership, and require a reusable playbook before expanding.

02 / HOW I THINK

Start with the constraint.
Then choose the move.

Explore three illustrative leadership situations. Each makes the priority, the trade-off, and the proof explicit.

THE SITUATION

People have access.
Behavior hasn’t changed.

A launch generated interest, but sustained use is weak. Another awareness campaign may amplify a workflow problem.

MY FIRST QUESTION

Where does the intended behavior break down?

01 / DIAGNOSE

Listen to users and champions. Map the moments between first exposure, first use, and repeated use.

02 / DECIDE

Fix the highest-friction workflow before expanding the campaign. Give the team a usable example and a named owner.

03 / PROVE IT

Track activation, repeat use, and time to first useful outcome against a defined baseline.

The trade-off

Less launch volume now; more evidence that the next wave can sustain adoption.

Illustrative decisions, not a client case study or a claim of measured campaign results.
